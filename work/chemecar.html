A 12 V battery from raw materials

ChemE Car vehicles run on power their teams make themselves. As part of UCI’s starting battery team, I helped design and build a 12 V lead-acid battery from raw materials.

Electrodes to electrolyte

Building a cell from scratch means getting every step right. That takes clean electrodes, a uniform deposited layer, the right electrolyte, and a pack that holds its potential under load.

  • Acid cleaning, electroplating and electrode preparationBuilt
  • Electrode material research and selectionTeam
  • Electrolyte composition optimized; additives to limit dendrite formationTeam
  • Charge and discharge behavior and efficiency evaluatedMeasured
  • Neutralization and buffer preparation, chemical storage and safetyPractice
  • Soldering and assembly of the 12 V packBuilt
