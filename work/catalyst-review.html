Fuel-cell catalyst literature review

A smaller research project in a chemistry laboratory, reviewing how platinum-based multimetallic nanoparticles are made and characterized for hydrogen fuel cells.

Chemistry laboratoryS-10
Type
Literature research with exposure to lab techniques
Subject
PtCoNiRuIr catalysts · High Temperature Liquid Shock Synthesis (HTLS)
Techniques
UV/VIS · FTIR · ICP-MS · SEM

What the literature reported

The reviewed work made multimetallic PtCoNiRuIr nanoparticles by High Temperature Liquid Shock Synthesis. It reported particles of about 3.24 nm, made rapidly, and used ICP-MS to check how the elements were distributed.

This was literature-based research. I reviewed these results; I did not synthesize the catalysts myself.
